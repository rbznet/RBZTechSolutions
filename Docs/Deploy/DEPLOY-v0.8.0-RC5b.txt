RBZ PC Health v0.8.0 RC5b - Customer Report Simplification

Patch script:
Tools\Patches\Apply-RC5b.ps1

Deploy notes:
Docs\Deploy\DEPLOY-v0.8.0-RC5b.txt

Changes:
- Customer report removes raw diagnostic Details.
- Adds plain-English wording for common customer-facing issues.
- Adds a category-level Health Summary.
- Customer report only shows actionable Issues & Recommendations.
- Removes full per-category Diagnostic Results from Customer Report.
- Technician Report keeps full technical detail.
- Removes the previous root-level Apply-RC5.ps1 after successful patching.

Run from repo root:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Tools\Patches\Apply-RC5b.ps1

Then launch RBZHealth.ps1 as Administrator and generate both Customer and Technician reports.
