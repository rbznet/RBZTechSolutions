RBZ PC Health v0.8.0 RC1 - Diagnostic Priority Engine

Baseline: committed/released v0.7.0.

Adds a conservative priority engine without changing existing finding Status
or the health-score calculation.

Install:
1. Extract this overlay into the existing RBZ-PC-Health repo.
2. Run:
   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Apply-RC1.ps1
3. Run:
   powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\RBZHealth.ps1

RC1 adds the engine only. RC2 will expose Top Issues / Recommended Actions in
the technician workflow.
