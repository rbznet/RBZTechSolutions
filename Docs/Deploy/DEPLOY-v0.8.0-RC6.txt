RBZ PC Health v0.8.0 RC6 - Technician Notes & Service Outcome

Baseline: GitHub main dc48b19

Patch:
Tools\Patches\Apply-RC6.ps1

Adds:
- Service Record tab between Before / After and Repair Centre.
- Customer Complaint / Issue Description.
- Technician Notes.
- Work Performed.
- Service Outcome.
- Further Recommendations / Customer Advice.
- Checkbox controlling customer-safe Service Summary.
- Technician Notes never appear in Customer Report.
- Full Service Record appears in Technician Report.
- Service notes are embedded in report JSON.
- Existing diagnostics/scoring/repair logic is unchanged.
- Repair Centre navigation is updated from tab index 4 to 5.
- No root-level patch or backup files are created.

Apply from repo root:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Tools\Patches\Apply-RC6.ps1

Test:
1. Launch RBZHealth.ps1 as Administrator.
2. Confirm Service Record tab appears.
3. Fill all five fields.
4. Run Full Scan.
5. Customer Report: service summary appears, but Technician Notes do not.
6. Technician Report: all five fields appear.
7. Untick customer summary and regenerate Customer Report: Service Summary is absent.
8. Technician Priorities > Open Repair Centre still opens Repair Centre.
9. Health score/findings remain unchanged.
