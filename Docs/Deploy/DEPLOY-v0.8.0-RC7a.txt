RBZ PC Health v0.8.0 RC7a - Service Record Sign-off Layout

Patch:
Tools\Patches\Apply-RC7a.ps1

Requires:
RC7 applied locally.

Change:
Moves Technician, Final Service Status, Completion Date/Time and Complete Service
to directly below the Service Record heading so the sign-off controls are visible
without scrolling to the bottom.

No report, diagnostic, scoring or Repair Centre logic changes.

Apply:
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Tools\Patches\Apply-RC7a.ps1

Test:
- Open Service Record and confirm sign-off controls are visible at the top.
- Confirm all note fields remain below.
- Select a final status and click Complete Service.
- Confirm timestamp fills in.
- Generate Customer and Technician reports to verify RC7 output is unchanged.
